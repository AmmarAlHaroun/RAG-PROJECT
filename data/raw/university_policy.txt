# University Policies

- Attendance:
  - Students are expected to attend scheduled classes and practical sessions.
  - Students should notify the instructor when an absence is unavoidable.
  - Students are responsible for reviewing material missed during an absence.
- Academic integrity:
  - Students must submit their own work unless collaboration is explicitly allowed.
  - Sources, data, code, and ideas from other people must be properly credited.
  - Plagiarism, fabrication, cheating, and unauthorized assistance are prohibited.
  - Suspected violations may be referred for academic review.
- Use of artificial intelligence:
  - AI tools may be used only when permitted by the instructor or assignment instructions.
  - Students remain responsible for the accuracy and originality of submitted work.
  - AI-generated material must be reviewed, corrected, and acknowledged when required.
  - Students must not upload confidential university or personal information to public AI tools.
- Assessment and grading:
  - Assignments must be submitted through the designated learning platform.
  - Students should check submission requirements before the deadline.
  - Grades are based on the published assessment criteria and rubric.
  - Grade questions should be submitted respectfully within the stated review period.
- Late work:
  - Late submissions may receive a penalty unless an extension has been approved.
  - Extension requests should be made before the deadline whenever possible.
  - Documented emergencies may be considered under university procedures.
- Communication:
  - Students should use their official university email for academic communication.
  - Messages should include the student name, course, and a clear description of the request.
  - Instructors normally respond during published working hours.
- Accessibility and accommodations:
  - Students who require academic accommodations should contact the authorized accessibility office.
  - Approved accommodations apply according to the official accommodation notice.
  - Course staff should protect the privacy of accommodation information.
- Privacy and data protection:
  - Personal information must be handled only for legitimate academic purposes.
  - Student records must not be shared with unauthorized people.
  - Research or project data must follow applicable university and legal requirements.
- Student conduct:
  - Students must treat classmates, instructors, and staff with respect.
  - Harassment, discrimination, threats, and disruptive behavior are not permitted.
  - Concerns may be reported through the university student-support or conduct channels.
- Safety:
  - Students must follow health, safety, and laboratory instructions.
  - Accidents, hazards, and security incidents should be reported promptly.
- Appeals:
  - Students may appeal academic or administrative decisions using the official university process.
  - Appeals must be submitted within the stated deadline and include supporting information.
